Figure 3, panel H. Scale-resolved spatial organization of c-FOS⁺. Left: for each animal, the observed number of positive–positive pairs closer than r, expressed in standard deviations from its own random-labelling null. Random labelling holds every cell position, stratum and positive count fixed and permutes only which cells are positive; the null mean and variance are exact, so field geometry cancels and no edge correction applies. Pairs are counted within all DAPI-associated centroids, by acquisition and tissue side. Thin lines are animals, thick lines are condition means, and the reference is 0. Right: the between-condition F at each radius with its global 95% envelope, obtained by exhaustive enumeration of 9240 animal-label allocations. The test is the studentized maximum deviation over the whole 20–150 µm range, so one p-value covers every distance: p=0.220, Holm p=0.220 with n=5/3/3 estimable animals. At these group sizes no exact test can return p<0.00022. The observed curve does not exit the envelope at any radius. The earlier scalar measures are retained and tested separately, with no joint test: density-contour enrichment: exact three-condition test over 9240 allocations, p=0.193, Holm p=0.580; Allulose–Sucrose within the shared platform, p=0.200, Holm p=0.600. clustering excess: exact three-condition test over 9240 allocations, p=0.459, Holm p=0.580; Allulose–Sucrose within the shared platform, p=0.200, Holm p=0.600. Proximity does not establish functional connectivity, and Water acquisition remains confounded with condition.
