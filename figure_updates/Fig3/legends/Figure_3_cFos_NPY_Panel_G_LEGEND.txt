Figure 3, panel G. Example from the bilateral Allulose hypothalamic field E8_FR6-4, also shown in D. The accepted registered display frame, external tissue outline and ventricular polygon are reused; the third ventricle (3V) is dorsal and the median eminence (ME) is ventral. These outlines are display annotations and do not change analysis regions or denominators. Gray points are NPY-positive centroids without c-FOS; orange points are c-FOS/NPY double-positive centroids. The 156 orange segments join every pair of activated cells closer than 20 um, the smallest radius the analysis sweeps. They are counted pairs, not anatomical connections, and proximity does not establish functional connectivity. At larger radii the counted pairs fill the field and the picture would stop showing structure. The four black dashed circles, centred on one activated cell marked in white, show four of the radii panels H and I sweep, at true scale and labelled above in um: 20, 70, 110, 150 um. Every activated cell inside a circle forms one counted pair with the central cell; for this cell the counts are 5, 17, 33, 43, and how they grow with r is what the curves in H and I summarise. The cell was chosen as the one with the median number of neighbours at the smallest radius among activated cells whose largest circle fits inside the displayed frame, so it is typical rather than selected for effect. The analysis repeats that count for every activated cell, not only the marked one, and within each tissue side. The accepted bilateral window displays 492 of 494 NPY centroids and all 131 double positives; two NPY-only centroids lie outside the original display crop and remain in the analysis. Scale bar, 100 um. This is a descriptive method example, not an individual statistical comparison.
