Figure 3. NPY-associated c-FOS labeling and spatial profiles in the arcuate region.

A, Representative NPY-GFP (green), c-FOS (red), merged and magnified merged images from water, sucrose and allulose animals. Dashed boxes locate the magnified fields; black outlines identify NPY ROIs assigned to double-positive DAPI nuclei. Scale bars, 100 µm in full fields and 50 µm in magnified fields. Display intensities are adjusted independently and do not support comparisons of brightness across conditions.
B, Water: DAPI nuclear masks displaying NPY-positive nuclei, c-FOS-positive nuclei and combined assignments. Visible NPY/c-FOS/double-positive counts are 205/113/57. Black ventricular boundaries were reviewed manually against the original registered DAPI. Gray external outlines are DAPI-supported tissue envelopes used only for presentation. Neither outline is an analytical ROI. Scale bars, 100 µm.
C, Sucrose, E7_FR7-5: DAPI nuclear masks displaying NPY-positive nuclei, c-FOS-positive nuclei and combined assignments. Visible NPY/c-FOS/double-positive counts are 292/66/19; full native-field counts are 302/81/19, with 3,241 DAPI nuclei. The display crop does not define the analytical denominator. Black ventricular boundaries were reviewed manually against the original registered DAPI. Gray external outlines are DAPI-supported tissue envelopes used only for presentation. Neither outline is an analytical ROI. Scale bars, 100 µm.
D, Allulose, E8_FR6-4: DAPI nuclear masks displaying NPY-positive nuclei, c-FOS-positive nuclei and combined assignments. Visible NPY/c-FOS/double-positive counts are 493/271/131. Black ventricular boundaries were reviewed manually against the original registered DAPI. Gray external outlines are DAPI-supported tissue envelopes used only for presentation. Neither outline is an analytical ROI. Scale bars, 100 µm.
E, c-FOS-positive nuclei as a percentage of DAPI nuclei. Counts are summed across each animal's fields/sections before computing its percentage; animals have equal weight. Water/Sucrose/Allulose: n=5/3/3. Circles denote animals from the original experiment; bars show mean ± SD. The within-condition 1.5×IQR rule was applied separately by endpoint to this experiment; no outliers were flagged and all 11 animals were retained. The Water group is far more dispersed than either sugar group and its spread coincides with acquisition batch, so the omnibus does not assume a common variance. Welch's robust F with its null enumerated over all 9240 distinct animal allocations: F=7.642, p=0.075. Brown–Forsythe test of equal variance: p=0.375. The Welch F approximation gives p=0.042; its degrees of freedom are estimated from three-animal variances and it is anti-conservative at these group sizes. Exact studentized permutation (Welch t over every animal-label allocation): Water–Sucrose: p=0.429; Water–Allulose: p=0.250; Sucrose–Allulose: p=0.100. At these group sizes no exact test can return p<0.1. Equal-variance one-way ANOVA (sensitivity): F=2.367, p=0.156. Two-sided Mann–Whitney tests use exact animal-label enumeration with average ranks for ties: Water–Sucrose: p=1.000; Water–Allulose: p=0.393; Sucrose–Allulose: p=0.100; p values are nominal, without adjustment, preserving the original abundance analysis. The pooled cohort/platform comparison is exploratory; condition and acquisition are incompletely balanced, so these comparisons do not isolate a treatment effect. Accepted cell ROIs and the Figure 3 association rules are retained; no new segmentation or ROI redrawing is performed.

G, Example from the bilateral Allulose hypothalamic field E8_FR6-4, also shown in D. The accepted registered display frame, external tissue outline and ventricular polygon are reused; the third ventricle (3V) is dorsal and the median eminence (ME) is ventral. These outlines are display annotations and do not change analysis regions or denominators. Gray points are NPY-positive centroids without c-FOS; orange points are c-FOS/NPY double-positive centroids. The 156 orange segments join every pair of activated cells closer than 20 um, the smallest radius the analysis sweeps. They are counted pairs, not anatomical connections, and proximity does not establish functional connectivity. At larger radii the counted pairs fill the field and the picture would stop showing structure. The four black dashed circles, centred on one activated cell marked in white, show four of the radii panels H and I sweep, at true scale and labelled above in um: 20, 70, 110, 150 um. Every activated cell inside a circle forms one counted pair with the central cell; for this cell the counts are 5, 17, 33, 43, and how they grow with r is what the curves in H and I summarise. The cell was chosen as the one with the median number of neighbours at the smallest radius among activated cells whose largest circle fits inside the displayed frame, so it is typical rather than selected for effect. The analysis repeats that count for every activated cell, not only the marked one, and within each tissue side. The accepted bilateral window displays 492 of 494 NPY centroids and all 131 double positives; two NPY-only centroids lie outside the original display crop and remain in the analysis. Scale bar, 100 um. This is a descriptive method example, not an individual statistical comparison.
H, Scale-resolved spatial organization of c-FOS⁺. Left: for each animal, the observed number of positive–positive pairs closer than r, expressed in standard deviations from its own random-labelling null. Random labelling holds every cell position, stratum and positive count fixed and permutes only which cells are positive; the null mean and variance are exact, so field geometry cancels and no edge correction applies. Pairs are counted within all DAPI-associated centroids, by acquisition and tissue side. Thin lines are animals, thick lines are condition means, and the reference is 0. Right: the between-condition F at each radius with its global 95% envelope, obtained by exhaustive enumeration of 9240 animal-label allocations. The test is the studentized maximum deviation over the whole 20–150 µm range, so one p-value covers every distance: p=0.220, Holm p=0.220 with n=5/3/3 estimable animals. At these group sizes no exact test can return p<0.00022. The observed curve does not exit the envelope at any radius. The earlier scalar measures are retained and tested separately, with no joint test: density-contour enrichment: exact three-condition test over 9240 allocations, p=0.193, Holm p=0.580; Allulose–Sucrose within the shared platform, p=0.200, Holm p=0.600. clustering excess: exact three-condition test over 9240 allocations, p=0.459, Holm p=0.580; Allulose–Sucrose within the shared platform, p=0.200, Holm p=0.600. Proximity does not establish functional connectivity, and Water acquisition remains confounded with condition.
