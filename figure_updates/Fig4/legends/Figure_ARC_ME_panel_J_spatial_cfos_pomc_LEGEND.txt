(J) Scale-resolved spatial organization of c-FOS⁺POMC⁺ in ARC. For each animal the number of activated-cell pairs closer than r is counted within POMC-positive centroids, by acquisition and tissue side, and expressed in standard deviations from its own random-labelling null; the null mean and variance are exact, so field geometry cancels. The independent unit is the cage: animal curves are averaged with equal weight within each cage. Left: thin lines are cages, thick lines are condition means, and the reference is 0. Right: the between-condition F at each radius with its global 95% envelope, from an exhaustive enumeration of 210 cage-label allocations. The test is the studentized maximum deviation over the whole 20–150 µm range, so one p-value covers every distance: p=0.714, Holm p=1.000 with 2/2/3 cages (13 animals). At these sizes no exact test can return p<0.010. The observed curve does not exit the envelope at any radius. The right-hand vertical axis is logarithmic: with two cages in one condition the envelope sits up to two orders of magnitude above the observed curve, which on a linear axis would be indistinguishable from the axis line. The median eminence is not analysed: it retains 74 marker-associated cells with 28 activated across 11 animals, and only 6 of those animals have two or more activated cells, too sparse for a curve. The method is identical to Figure 3 panels H and I; only the population and the experimental unit differ. Proximity does not establish functional connectivity.
