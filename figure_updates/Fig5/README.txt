HIL-reviewed DAPI/Iba1 maintenance
-------------------------------
The normal figure build verifies and consumes the shipped HIL-reviewed masks. To
regenerate them without changing raw_data:

  /home/server/anaconda3/envs/cellpose_env/bin/python \
    Fig5/03_resegment_dapi_gpu.py --gpu-device 0
  "$PAPER_PYTHON" Fig5/04_regenerate_iba1_from_reviewed_dapi.py

The first command requires CUDA and uses dapi_channel. The second uses those
HIL-reviewed DAPI nuclei as seeds for Iba1 watershed instances. Both commands are
resumable and write TIFF, NPY, QC PNG, CSV, and JSON receipts only below
hil_review/. DAPI/Iba1 TIFF decoding and manifest hashes pass; if Cellpose GUI
rejects one of these raw TIFF names, the likely cause is its adjacent historical
non-GUI *_seg.npy sidecar. Do not repair or overwrite raw_data to work around a
GUI auto-load issue.

Cell and soma mask review, then classification
----------------------------------------------
The active reviewer uses Iba1-driven cell and soma mask proposals rebuilt for all
61 sections under analyses/Fig5/results/soma_refined_20260907_v1. It finds locally
prominent Iba1 soma candidates, checks DAPI association, and grows masks within
the Iba1 signal. Existing accepted anatomy, raw TIFFs, and prior masks are kept.
The new masks are proposals requiring human review, not publication-final masks.

Launch or resume from the Paper root:

  /home/server/anaconda3/envs/paper_apotome_repro/bin/python \
    Fig5/08_review_microglia_masks.py --port 8765

Open http://127.0.0.1:8765. The active batch is
hil_review/microglia_masks_20260907_v2: 300 cells from 15 animals, including 75
per proposed class. Cyan shows the cell/process mask and amber shows the soma.
Use Paint/Erase soma or Paint/Erase cell/processes on the right image to correct
actual masks. Save mask commits an edit; choosing a morphology class saves the
current mask and class together. Keys 1-4 choose the class, and S skips uncertain
cells. The unchanged Iba1 signal is retained when a mask is edited. Corrected
binary masks, source crop coordinates, revision histories, and CNN patches are
saved. Stale edits are rejected. Editing an already labelled mask requires
confirming its morphology class again.

The 34 labels from microglia_classes_20260906_v1 remain intact and are copied to
previous_review_labels_preserved.csv in the new review. Twenty-six nearby
resegmented targets are included first for reassessment; spatial proximity is
not treated as proof of cell identity. Previous labels are not silently assigned
to changed masks. The remaining original labels remain in the preserved file.

After at least 200 labels (>=12 in every class and >=3 animals), Finish review &
retrain starts supervised CNN training. It starts automatically at 300 labels
when class coverage is sufficient. Training verifies image and mask hashes and
uses the exact corrected mask patches accepted with the labels. Separate animals
are held out for validation, which also guides early stopping. Check
training_status.json for the current output_dir and log paths. The first run
remains under retrained/; the current corrected run is under
retrained_bncal_20260907_v3/ (the capped 25-epoch run is preserved as v2). With small review sets, the trainer recalculates CNN
batch-normalization statistics from training-animal patches before validation.
Validation animals never enter that calibration; all optimization settings and
the split remain fixed. Source code, hashes, configuration, and the chosen epoch
are saved with the model. Figure 5 publication files are not automatically
replaced by the candidate.

Rebuild the mask proposals into a new, absent directory:

  /home/server/anaconda3/envs/paper_apotome_repro/bin/python \
    Fig5/07_refine_microglia_masks.py --output-dir /tmp/fig5_new_mask_proposals


Reviewed classifier improvement experiment (2026-09-07)
-------------------------------------------------------
Fig5/09_improve_microglia_classifier.py now supports close target crops plus wider
branch context, frozen pretrained DINOv2 ViT-S/14 image features, 35 cell/soma
mask and intensity measurements, and nested whole-animal validation. Only the
203 confirmed human labels train the classifiers. Model selection, normalization
and PCA stay inside the training animals. All 14,415 source cells have reusable
image features; the original images, accepted masks and labels are preserved.

Results and saved candidates:
  analyses/Fig5/results/transfer_reviewed_20260907_v1/

The requested publication supplement is now FigS6/Figure_S6.pdf and .png.
It compares 73.9% versus 57.0% balanced accuracy on the same 203 reviews;
FigS6/README.txt documents its frozen inputs and independent reproduction.

On the historical 40 validation cells, DINOv2 balanced accuracy is 69.9% versus
59.5% for the previous supervised CNN; the selected shape model reaches 76.1%.
The broader, nested animal-held-out evaluation gives DINOv2 57.0%, the selected
learning procedure 69.8%, and existing morphology proposals 73.9%. This does not
establish improvement over the existing proposals. These remain development
candidates and have not replaced the promoted Figure 5 classifier or panels.
The historical 40-cell result is not an independent final test.

Fig5/10_audit_microglia_transfer.py independently verifies animal separation,
recomputes metrics, refits the first animal fold, reproduces final predictions,
and prepares 300 additional unlabeled targets across 15 animals. The priority
queue targets disagreements/uncertainty and must not be treated as an unbiased
test sample. Existing human labels are excluded from that queue.

Portable current choices and optional independent review
-------------------------------------------------------
Normal reproduction reuses the saved 203 human choices (50 Ramified, 32 Rod-like,
50 Activated, 71 Amoeboid) and their saved trained candidate. It opens no reviewer.
The frozen microglial_review_data/ bundle includes original labels, 300 review
crops and cell/soma/DAPI masks, all 14,415 CNN crops and soma masks, source tables,

Use the Python interpreter from the scientific paper environment. From Paper:

  python Fig5/12_microglial_choices.py --output-dir /tmp/microglial_current_choices

  ./reproduce_all_figures.sh --do_microglial_choices

Or run only the optional review/training branch:

  python Fig5/12_microglial_choices.py --do_microglial_choices \
    --output-dir /tmp/microglial_new_choices

Open MICROGLIAL_REVIEW_URL from the terminal. The new session contains the same
300 targets with zero prefilled human choices. The current 203 labels stay intact.
Finish after 200-300 labels with class/animal coverage; training starts
automatically at 300 and the reproduction continues after training completes.
To resume a saved independent session, add --review-dir with its printed path
and choose a fresh --output-dir. --prepare-only creates a session without serving
or training, and --validate-only checks the frozen bundle without writing.

Choices, a reuse/training receipt and the candidate are installed to
analyses/Fig5/results/current_microglial_choices/ during full reproduction.
These are the review/classifier branch; the accepted Figure 5 publication panels

Classifier gate
---------------
Use Fig5/01_analyze_gfap_iba1_microglia.py --prepare-labels-only to create a
fresh labeling template, perform independent HIL review of the cells, then provide the
completed labels with --microglia-labels-csv and supervised classifier mode.
Cells and sections are never biological replicates; animal-level inference is
required and inferential comparisons require at least three animals per group.
