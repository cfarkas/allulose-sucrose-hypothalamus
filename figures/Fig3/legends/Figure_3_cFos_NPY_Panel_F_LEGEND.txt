Figure 3, panel F. c-FOS/NPY double-positive nuclei as a percentage of NPY-positive nuclei. Counts are summed across each animal's fields/sections before computing its percentage; animals have equal weight. Water/Sucrose/Allulose: n=5/3/3. Circles denote animals from the original experiment; bars show mean ± SD. The within-condition 1.5×IQR rule was applied separately by endpoint to this experiment; no outliers were flagged and all 11 animals were retained. The Water group is far more dispersed than either sugar group and its spread coincides with acquisition batch, so the omnibus does not assume a common variance. Welch's robust F with its null enumerated over all 9240 distinct animal allocations: F=82.34, p=0.017. Brown–Forsythe test of equal variance: p=0.332. The Welch F approximation gives p=0.00021; its degrees of freedom are estimated from three-animal variances and it is anti-conservative at these group sizes. Exact studentized permutation (Welch t over every animal-label allocation): Water–Sucrose: p=0.464; Water–Allulose: p=0.196; Sucrose–Allulose: p=0.100. At these group sizes no exact test can return p<0.1. Equal-variance one-way ANOVA (sensitivity): F=2.548, p=0.139. Two-sided Mann–Whitney tests use exact animal-label enumeration with average ranks for ties: Water–Sucrose: p=0.786; Water–Allulose: p=0.786; Sucrose–Allulose: p=0.100; p values are nominal, without adjustment, preserving the original abundance analysis. The pooled cohort/platform comparison is exploratory; condition and acquisition are incompletely balanced, so these comparisons do not isolate a treatment effect. Accepted cell ROIs and the Figure 3 association rules are retained; no new segmentation or ROI redrawing is performed.
