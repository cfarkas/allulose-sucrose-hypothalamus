(F) Vertical channel-separated analysis-mask cartoons from the exact panel A field (FR722, S03; source bounds 0,0,960,720). DAPI nuclei are blue (6,290 labeled instances; 217,058 positive pixels), final QC-accepted Iba1-positive cells are red-magenta (397 labeled cells; 31,913 positive pixels), and the final threshold-derived GFAP-positive signal is green (9,273 positive pixels; pixels, not cells). Every channel uses the same DAPI-derived tissue visualization envelope and the same pixel-identical HIL-accepted/final ARC, ME, and VMN mask; the tissue envelope is visualization support, not an anatomical ROI or quantitative denominator.

ARC, ME, and VMN are shown in canonical annotation-code order in all quantitative panels. Bars are animal means and error bars are SD; circles are individual biological animals and crosses identify values outside within-condition Tukey 1.5-IQR fences. All finite animal values, including marked outliers, were retained. Primary global values are ordinary one-way ANOVA p values. Pairwise W-S, W-A, and S-A values are raw exact two-sided Mann–Whitney U p values. Cage sensitivity uses unweighted cage means with one-way ANOVA and exact MWU. Panel H also uses raw exact cage-label binomial deviance on cage-summed activated/total microglia counts. Positive low denominators are retained; a zero denominator is an intrinsically undefined animal ratio but its 0/0 counts remain in the cage sum. No outcome-dependent filtering is used (W, Water; S, Sucrose; A, Allulose). Animals available anywhere in the dataset were Water n=3, Sucrose n=6, and Allulose n=6; actual finite animal Ns can be smaller for an endpoint/region.
