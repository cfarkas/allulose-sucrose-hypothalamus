FROZEN MICROGLIAL CHOICES AND CLASSIFIER INPUTS

203 genuine saved human classifications, 300 review crops with cell/soma/DAPI
masks, 14,415 original CNN patches and soma masks, source measurement/proposal
tables, and the saved human-trained CNN candidate. No new microscopy was acquired.
All files are original bytes; the manifest binds each ZIP and member by SHA-256.

Use Fig5/12_microglial_choices.py to verify/reuse these choices or initialize a
separate blank review with --do_microglial_choices. Portable initialization
rewrites only working-copy paths, with a receipt, and preserves the frozen labels.
The archived candidate has 59.5% balanced accuracy on its 40-cell development
validation split. Figure S6 separately reports the 203-cell morphology/DINOv2
comparison; these validation designs must not be conflated.
